# Code of conduct

TelegramAgentBot welcomes people who participate in good faith and help keep
the project useful, safe, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code, documentation, and ideas.
- Assume good intent while still addressing concrete technical or safety risks.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Give actionable feedback and allow reasonable time for a response.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish credentials, private prompts, transcripts,
  personal information, or private repository details.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest and respect project licensing.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language or unwanted attention;
- doxxing or deliberate disclosure of private information;
- impersonation or misleading claims about authority, review, or affiliation;
- repeated disruption, spam, or off-topic promotion;
- publishing exploit details or credentials against the security policy;
- retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this policy.
Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, repetition, and the
safety of affected people.

For public GitHub abuse, use GitHub's report and block tools. For a
project-specific concern, contact a maintainer through the repository owner's
published GitHub profile contact method. Do not place sensitive personal or
security details in a public issue.

Good-faith reports will be handled as privately and fairly as the available
channels allow. This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull
requests, reviews, and other project spaces managed by the maintainers.
